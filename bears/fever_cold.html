fever cold

when the fever's cooled,
the body grows a heart
of copper and steel wire.

the empty stands engulfed
by an electrical tremor,
inner solar winds,
make the water tremble.

walls under some skin
hum and beat in rhythm
and the only quiet
they deserve
is feeling lost.

this cold,
the nerve.
        my face
        my jaw

the sore mouth stands
before the grail or wine of glass,
detached, conscientiously steps,
with teeth, three missing from the top,
and clings to the margins of the cup,
smilingly accepts the shards.

a beautiful goose dreams to fly away
and become a swan. it wanted a long neck,
or maybe two, to intertwine. as the butcher's
love grew cold, it broke the window pane,
outside.

the air was still cold, and the pencil leg
broken at its half. a purring, happy cat
rested its head between the bent legs
and ate towards the heart, bottom-up.
