[sweet pinnochio]

sweet pinnochio:
your beak is a weapon.

down the road
between gas stations

i wonder if you ever
look through the glass

and see us there
like two caged animals

between stone walls
how i bent like a reed

trying to climb
up the stream

te piși pe poeziile mele
te piși pe ele.

february 2026
