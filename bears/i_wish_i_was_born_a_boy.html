i wish i was born a boy

i wish i was born a boy
i would have been your boy

and i'd look in your unhappy eyes,
at your teeth - sweet as charcoal,

and plant a touch on your head
that's been fermenting in my lips

 for ages,

without shame. dar cu frică.
cu credință și cu dragoste.

i ask you to stay alert. because
you've turned your back to the

fire and if you're not careful
its flame will mark your spotty

spine.

but until then i'll calculate the
way my limbs hang from my

tree trunk middle so that they
hang just as the branches loaded

with pomegranates you like
hang over the mud where you

fed me peaches.

september 2023
