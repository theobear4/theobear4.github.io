[i stand in the sun]

i wait in the sun
    hoping to bloom

    it bears my eyes shut

and braids my hair
    together with my fingers

 it forms a leather bracelet
    out of my wrongs and my rights,
    
black leather on ply-wood skies.
    orange-lit tobacco,

     long way to fly.

    i wrote a letter one year ago
but the ink hasn’t dried.

i imagine myself purple / i imagine myself warm.
i imagine myself just peel and core.
i imagine myself wooden / i imagine myself small.

     to protect from the cold
burn all your clothes

     make yourself float in dark water
and watch the smoking shore move, engulf.

    i press my chest against the sea floor
to make my ribs bruised and back sore.

19 june 2025
