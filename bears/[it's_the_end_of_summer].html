[it's the end of summer]

it’s the end of summer and i’m wearing
the shirt you cut myself in

(did you wash the blood
off its hem)

i’m mistaken for two different names
and none of them belong to me. my nails turn blue

in the morning
pink at night.

i wish that the leaves here turned yellow from green
but it’s just the sheer layer of skin over ragged train tracks

that changes its color.
the space bends around

you and me and the strip of all the lies you whispered in me
wraps around my head like a bow, take it and rip it and hang me

from my ceiling with it,
hang me until i drip and make

a low bridge over your nose.

september 2023
