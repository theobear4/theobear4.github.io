[he said how about we play]

he said how about we play
a game something like an experiment
we'll go back in time two years
it's arbitrary it could be three or one
you will see yourself as i've seen you
with your backside towards my face
these willed pink-tipped flowers
i'll use to carress your face your neck
your hair your eyes must be closed
with my hands i'll pry open your mouth
and stick the dried stems down your lips
into your throat and leave them there take
a step back and admire the vase of your
cranium but then i'll leave and the years
will pass and it will be your job to hinge
your jaw open when it surely locks because
those petals cannot fall and die i trust in
you that you will keep them alive.
