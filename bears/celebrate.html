celebrate

all i know are mistakes and today i’ve made as many as i’ve taken breaths.

even though i’ve had [more than] enough time to sleep, there is
nectar under the glass misted shattered under my eyes.

when i shut my skin i see a celestial sky, an echo, a reflection of the moon lashed in water

[remember the lake remember the grass]

pleading again

you precious jewel
you conqueror
you Man
you loving heart

uncreated and free

i’m your brother

i’m asking you to
tear my heart out
i’ve asked five times

but you didn’t hear

and you smiled warmly with my soul between your teeth.

you belong with the birds
[and i’m your brother, the maggot, the fly, the common houselfy]

so i’ve recited you name, red one-hundred syllable prayer,
i’ve thrown it in the sound reverberating in my ear

last year and this year i’m a room without sleep
tired of dying

and the only thing, still floating after you’re gone and i’m me only me still me
is a whispered memory descending in my navel, little white river.

february 2024
