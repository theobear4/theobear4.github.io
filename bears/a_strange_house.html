a strange house

there is a strange
house and i
went looking for
your name because
i can’t remember it.

there is three of you there is a strange house and your name.
(i sinned first so there is no need to worry, i split in two, i split in the middle do you understand, at the bottom of my belly do you see it, it’s splitting me alive)

there is three of you and all of you are the moon. who do you love? who do you love? you’re something i don’t even have a name for, only tremble, murmur you out like a prayer but there are no words. moon-light made scars on your skin and my lips travel to each one.

i just wanted
to know the worst.

in the strange house there were more of you
i fell down the stairs but luckily one of you was there to help me up but instead of helping me up you pushed me down and the circular staircase kept going like a fracture i fell and fell and fell

when you finally got to me you asked me to get closer so i kissed you legs. because there was nothing left to lose i opened my mouth and everything fell out and everything was lost. wind in my hair and my untied hair in the wind and a black sky black hands black laughter.

in the strange house i remember the carpets were persian and the boys were burning. i told you i’m willing to die for love but this isn’t it. this is eating the sweet core of the sunflower seed and spitting out the shell, only letting it lay on your tongue enough for the sale to seep and leave again on my cheeks.

stories of cats and dogs and knives and long black sleeves and it’s all coming back to me, even charcoal eyes, even peaches. even spiders. still spiders. the sound of skin against skin, the sound of bruises, the sound of teeth against teeth. how i got used to it. how i wanted it to be more than hunger.

i should die in water, all i want is to die in this water. but before i do tell me which dress you like best, which one the water will make flow like blood rushing in a bathtub like smoke on a rooftop. i take this water in like a blessing, like a stream of sunset light, like a knee to the face. like a face to the knee.

me and my lover are making out in a strange house. but i’m in pain so i can’t understand him, none of him, i can’t tell whose tongue is whose and why my skin feels so hot why i feel like i’m burning when i’m all alone and my lover isn’t even here. he’s back at the hotel room. i was waiting.

i fear my heart is too small and you’re the biggest dragonfly i’ve ever met, the biggest spider waiting in the corner next to my mattress. a butterfly blade breathing and begging for a soft belly. i got unlucky, i know. what’s left is not a scar but a tenderness under my forehead.

i feel like i’ve done something horrible like swallowed a whole bottle of pills or dug a tunnel across the whole yard just with my hands and my nails, and now my nails are gone, they’re gone completely. and it was raining so the mud was soft but the rocks still weren’t. the grass was drowning and i couldn’t save it.

and those rocks are tied now to your ankles and they’re leading you down to the bottom of the river, something the water is wood-hard but at times itțs just like laying down on a couch soft, and the stream builds a beehive in the space between the end of your hips and the beginning of your ribcage.

your hands river-live.
i untie my hair.
hands close bones
closer. the mud
behind my eyes
is still soft and
waiting (want
ing). i feel it
pour and spin.
i’m only soft
when you’re
inside me, like
water. i can
only love like
a mouse you
know that i love
you, that i really do.
i love the knee
and i won’t let
it go. it’s my knee anyways.
it’s my own face.
