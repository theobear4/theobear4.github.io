[tissue paper skin]

tissue paper skin
tears asunder under a lamppost.
glowing barley head of hair
emanating waves of smoke

        — and the smoke engulfs.

let us play a game
where one of us is a killer,
the other, rightfully, killed.
cause of death etched in stone :

        drowned by the smoke.

time is a zone
and the past is a yellow house
on the roof, cat and mouse
lovingly play a ruleless game.

my heart is a root
deep under a fern tree
a titmouse on the foliage sings
as the cat goes for the kill.

july 2026
