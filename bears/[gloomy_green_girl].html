[gloomy green girl]

gloomy green girl
naked and awaiting a
gentlemanly figure to
undo the bow in your hair

silent and smiling for
an eternity. blue turned yellow
under your eyes, like a forgotten
bruise.

let there be music, he said.
filling up a stale room, purple disc
of vinyl and skin. but you’ve made sure
your hair is down and covering your

small breast. you look clumsy and
childish with the red lipstick over
the line of cupid’s bow. nothing more
than the muddy rib of Man you wait.

october 2023
