grieving after myself. i lost my soul going down a circular staircase

“you hate yourself”, was your explanation to the silence.
i haven’t been whole in years.

and so i flowed, i poured completely. i sometimes
wish you were here just to break my collarbones.
press on my ribs painfully until they wrap around you.

i’m only soft when you’re inside me — like water.
recently i’ve grown into a new shallowness.

one day doubling the month, i remember it was
the first day i went a whole day without eating
anything for the entire day. and it was may.

i can’t tell what i am so tell me i’m soft, not hard
tender not tough. soft soft and soft hot skin.

i feel the liquor fumes as soon as i wake up on my breath
although i haven’t drank in days. in the mirror my
eyes look like two cigar burns in an acrylic blanket.

how sad, your hands are too full to hold me in them
for just another minute. i can’t finish this, i’m sorry.

june 2024
