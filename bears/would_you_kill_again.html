would you kill again

would you       kill again
if you got       away with
killing once?       there is a
cat hitting its       head against
your chest,       begging you
to stay awake.       there is a
dream-dog       blonde,
licking the       salt from
your lips.       you killed
that poor dog       you left your
mark on its       lungs of mesh.
the heart of       a dog has
four rooms and       no bones. my
heart has one       room, and that
room is without       sleep, but with
a wooden bed       and a wooden
wall and a TV       that was old
before it ever       got the chance
to be young       and bars of
soap that never       cleaned a thing.
my heart has       a few bones too.
clovers, always       ripped apart,
by me, of course       who wishes.
but a crash       test car is
built to       be crashed.

november 2024
