juliette

you never know what to say.

i looked up at the soft ceiling and wished i was up there instead of down here
and i wished i was a dead birdlet fallen out of my nest

and for my lanky wings
to be discovered by a pair of curious girl-eyes.

i wished i was a small spider, as small as the space between your eyelashes,
secreting slimy web onto a freshly painted canvas.

i wished my little long legs would get stained by the fresh layer of prussian blue oil
(not denim blue) that will stop reflecting the light bouncing from your curls.

i’ll travel over the lapis lazuli dust mixed with egg yolks
and over the lead white that we decided to switch for titanium white
because i would feel the metal fumes up to my throat.

but i can’t be a bird and i can’t be a spider.
i’m something pretending to be a girl.

i have an earwig gnawing at my temples from the inside
dying to leave the hollow of my cheeks
drilling holes into airy teeth,
just to drink from the teary dew
on the perky forget-me-nots
just below my neck.

and those same old salt droplets made their way on my cheek
mixing with the sweat of your chest.

but i forgave you because you didn’t know what you were doing

and i didn’t know why i was crying.

july 2023
