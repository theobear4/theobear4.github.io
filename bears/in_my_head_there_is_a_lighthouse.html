[in my head there is a lighthouse]

in my head there is a lighthouse
and i am the children on a boat.
once in a while i am remembered
by an orange light.
    
    spectator to that shore
    breathing sea
    orange memory
    bores into me

brustling through crying weeds
i felt the blight spread about
on my elbows and my knees

july 2025
